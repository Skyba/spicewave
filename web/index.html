A focus timer

The ring at top right is a pomodoro timer: work for 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break, repeat. It starts the next round by itself. Tap it to pause, hold it to change the times or turn it off.

I keep this open next to my work as my pomodoro. Feel free to use it however you like.
